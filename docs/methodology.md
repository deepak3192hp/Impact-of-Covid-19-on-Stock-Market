# Research methodology

## Scope

The study examines the relationship between COVID-19 indicators and stock-market movement across six countries during the original observation period described in the project report.

## Proposed reproducible workflow

1. Document the selected countries, indices, symbols, and observation dates.
2. Record the source and license of every dataset.
3. Validate date formats, missing values, duplicate records, and units.
4. Align pandemic and market observations by date.
5. Calculate returns and other clearly documented features.
6. Establish a simple baseline before comparing machine-learning models.
7. Use time-aware train/validation/test splits to avoid leakage.
8. Report MAE, RMSE, and directional accuracy where appropriate.
9. Visualize results and discuss uncertainty, limitations, and possible confounding factors.

## Interpretation

A relationship between case counts, deaths, lockdowns, and index movement should not automatically be interpreted as causal. Market prices are affected by many factors, including monetary policy, fiscal stimulus, investor sentiment, company results, and global events.

The report is historical research and should not be used as an investment strategy or current market forecast.
